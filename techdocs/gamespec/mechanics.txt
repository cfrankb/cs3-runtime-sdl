game mechanic overview
=========================================================================
any consumable              01-1F and 40-4F 
(pickup trigger)            when assigned an attribute will make matching
                            tiles with the same attribute disapear

diamonds                    must be collected to complete the level
chute                       drops to the next level (same X,Y coordonates)
ice cubes                   can be pushed onto monster
exploding barrels           can damage up nearby enemies and player
water / swamp               health damage to player
boat                        immunity to water (1 time consumable)
boulder                     can be moved
dragon egg                  spawn dragon 
keys and doors              allow/block player (deny access)
monsters                    regular mobs are variations of basic AI
vine                        damage player, can swallow other monsters
                            and spread through water
blue mushroom               extra life (1 time consumable)
green mushroom              TBD
red mushroom                invincibility/god mode (1 time consumable)
yellow mushroom             sugar rush  (1 time consumable)
fruits                      give player sugar rush
scrolls                     messages (lore)
treasures                   gold/points
stop                        deny monster access 

level enter/exit            define where the player start and exit. 
                            if exit is defined player must exit at X,Y
                            either use start position or player tile to mark spawn point
